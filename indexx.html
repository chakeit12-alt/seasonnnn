Decentralized ≠ 100% Safe

With no intermediary, all responsibility falls on the user.
  • Lost Private Key / Seed Phrase: no call center can recover it
  • Phishing & Scams: tricked into clicking fake links or signing malicious approvals
  • Smart Contract Exploits: code vulnerabilities let hackers drain funds
  • Exchange Risk: the intermediary may go bankrupt or freeze withdrawals
  • User Errors: sending on the wrong network or mistyping an address cannot be undone

Crypto Risk Is More Than "Price Drops"

5 risks of digital assets

1 Market Risk

Many times more volatile than stocks, moving 20-50% up or down in a short time due to news or the global economy.

2 Liquidity Risk

Some altcoins and NFTs have low trading volume, so there may be no buyers when you sell (high Slippage).

3 Technology Risk

Blockchains or Smart Contracts may have vulnerabilities, leading to DeFi attacks and lost pool funds.

4 Cybersecurity Risk

Phishing sites, password-stealing malware, hacked accounts, or being tricked into signing malicious transactions.

5 Custody Risk

Assets left with an Exchange intermediary may be unrecoverable if it goes bankrupt or is defrauded.

What Does a Wallet Do?

Clearing up Wallet misconceptions
误 Myth

A Wallet is a "bag" that holds your coins inside

Lose your phone = lose your coins

实 Fact

Coins are not inside the Wallet; they are always recorded on the Blockchain (Public Ledger).

If you lose your phone, the coins remain. With your Seed Phrase / Private Key you can restore them on a new device.

Hot Wallet vs Cold Wallet: Convenience vs Security

Hot Wallet (Online)

Easy access and internet-connected. Good for trading, but higher risk.

Cold Wallet (Offline)

Highly secure and disconnected, protecting against hacks. Good for long-term storage.

  2. Use strong passwordsSet a different password for every account
  3. Always enable 2FAAdds another layer of security
  4. Beware of PhishingDon't click suspicious links
